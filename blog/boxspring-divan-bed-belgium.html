Boxspring or Divan Bed – What's the Difference?

Here is the honest answer most shops won't give you plainly: a boxspring and a divan bed are, in everyday Belgian and Dutch use, the same category of bed. Both are built on a solid, upholstered base — not an open frame with visible slats. The word you see just depends on language and tradition.

"Boxspring" is the term used across the Netherlands and Flanders. It describes a bed where the base (the "box") contains springs — traditionally bonell springs, now more often pocket springs — with a mattress on top and often a topper.

"Divan bed" is the British English term for the same idea: an upholstered base with a mattress and headboard sold as one set. The main practical difference you'll see is that classic divan bases often have storage drawers, while some boxsprings focus purely on the sprung-comfort layers. At Vantage Home our divan boxspring beds give you both — the upholstered sprung base and optional storage drawers.

In short: If you're searching "boxspring kopen" or "divan bed Belgium," you're looking at the same product. Pick based on the base, the storage, and the mattress — not the label.
