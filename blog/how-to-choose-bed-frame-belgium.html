How to Choose a Bed Frame in Belgium – Complete 2026 Guide

Published July 6, 2026  ·  By Vantage Home  ·  8 min read

Buying a bed frame in Belgium does not have to be complicated. Whether you are looking for a divan with storage drawers, an elegant sleigh bed, or a dramatic butterfly headboard — this guide covers everything you need to know before you buy, including sizes, prices, storage options and delivery.

1. Bed Frame Sizes in Belgium

Belgium uses the same standard mattress sizes as the rest of continental Europe. Unlike the UK, Belgian beds are measured in centimetres and all follow the standard 200 cm length. Here are the four sizes you will encounter:

Name Size (cm) Best for
Single 90 × 200 Children, studio apartments, guest rooms
Double 140 × 200 Couples in smaller bedrooms
King 160 × 200 Couples who want more space
Super King 180 × 200 Maximum comfort, large bedrooms

Important: Before ordering, measure your bedroom and leave at least 60 cm of clearance on each side of the bed and 90 cm at the foot end. Most Belgian apartments are well suited to a 140 × 200 or 160 × 200 bed.

2. The 4 Main Bed Frame Styles

There are four upholstered bed frame styles that are most popular in Belgium right now. Each has a distinct look and suits different bedroom styles.

Divan Bed

The divan bed is the most popular bed frame style in Belgium. It features a padded upholstered base with a matching headboard and is available with or without storage drawers. The design is clean and minimal, which makes it ideal for modern Belgian apartments. Divan beds are available in single, double, king and super king sizes and typically start from €355 including mattress at Vantage Home.

Panel Bed

A panel bed (also called a platform bed) features a low-profile frame with vertical panel lines running along the headboard and base. It has a more contemporary and architectural look than a divan bed. Panel beds typically have a gas-lift storage base that opens from below, providing generous under-bed storage. Starting from €450 including mattress.

Sleigh Bed

The sleigh bed gets its name from its distinctive curved headboard and footboard, which resemble the shape of a traditional horse-drawn sleigh. Originally a French neoclassical design from the 1820s, the sleigh bed has remained popular for two centuries because it combines classic elegance with modern comfort. Velvet sleigh beds are particularly popular in Belgian and Dutch bedrooms right now. Prices start from €475 including mattress.

Butterfly Bed

The butterfly bed is the most dramatic option available. It features a large winged headboard that flares outward at the top — like butterfly wings — creating a striking focal point in any bedroom. The butterfly bed suits people who want their bedroom to feel luxurious and impressive. It pairs well with neutral wall colours and minimal furniture. Prices start from €520 including mattress at Vantage Home.

Which style is right for you? If you want something clean and versatile, choose a divan. If you want storage and a modern look, choose a panel bed. If you love classic elegance, choose a sleigh bed. If you want maximum impact, choose a butterfly bed.

3. Storage Options Explained

One of the biggest advantages of modern bed frames in Belgium is the built-in storage. If your bedroom is small — as many Belgian apartment bedrooms are — a storage bed can replace the need for a separate chest of drawers or wardrobe.

Drawer Storage

Divan beds typically come with 2 or 4 storage drawers built into the base. Two-drawer beds have drawers on one side only — useful if the other side is against a wall. Four-drawer beds have 2 drawers on each side and provide more total storage capacity. Drawers are ideal for storing extra bedding, seasonal clothing and pillows.

Gas-Lift Storage

Panel, sleigh and butterfly beds typically use a gas-lift mechanism instead of drawers. You lift the mattress from the foot end and the entire base opens upward, revealing a large storage area underneath — typically 50–70 cm deep. Gas-lift beds provide significantly more storage than drawer beds and are ideal for storing larger items such as suitcases, duvets and boxes.

No Storage

If storage is not a priority, you can choose a bed frame without storage. These are typically slightly lower in price and have a cleaner, lower-profile look.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What bed frame sizes are standard in Belgium?

Standard Belgian bed sizes are Single (90×200 cm), Double (140×200 cm), King (160×200 cm) and Super King (180×200 cm). All follow the European standard of 200 cm length.

What is the difference between a divan bed and a sleigh bed?

A divan bed has a padded upholstered base with optional drawers and a simple padded headboard. A sleigh bed has a distinctive curved headboard and footboard for a more classical, elegant look. Both are available in velvet and come with a mattress included.

Do bed frames in Belgium include a mattress?

At Vantage Home, all bed frames include a quality mattress at no extra cost. You do not need to purchase a mattress separately. This makes our pricing significantly more competitive than retailers who sell the frame and mattress separately.

What is a gas-lift bed?

A gas-lift bed uses a hydraulic mechanism to lift the entire mattress and base upward, revealing a large storage space underneath. It provides significantly more storage than drawer-based beds and is ideal for Belgian apartments with limited space.
