Bed Frames, Sofas & Dining Tables — Delivered to Your Door Across Europe

Why We Built Vantage Home

Buying furniture should feel exciting, not exhausting. For too long, people across Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg were stuck choosing between expensive showroom pieces or low-quality options that needed replacing within a year. We saw that gap and did something about it.

At Vantage Home, every product we carry — from upholstered bed frames and beds with storage drawers to corner sofas, modular sectional sofas and extendable dining table sets — is sourced for build quality and real-world durability. These aren't display pieces. They're furniture made to handle daily family life.

Cash on Delivery isn't just a payment option for us — it's a statement. We get paid when you're satisfied with what arrives. That keeps us honest, and it keeps our standards high. Customers across Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany trust us with that promise.

What We Sell — Sizes, Prices & What to Expect

Our bed range covers divan beds, panel bed frames (platform style), sleigh beds, and butterfly beds — all available with optional gas-lift storage base and drawers. Sizes run from single 90×200 cm up to super king 180×200 cm, with or without a mattress. Prices start from €355 for a complete divan set including mattress.

For the living room, our 3-seater sofa bed doubles as a guest bed with hidden storage (from €480), and our 7-seater L-shape sectional sofa with adjustable headrests is one of our most popular pieces across Belgium and the Netherlands (€700, all countries). Both are available in multiple fabric colours.

Our dining table sets seat six — available in a Turkish-style printed glass design or a luxury extendable black-and-gold finish. Wardrobes come in sliding mirror and 3-door mirror styles from €450. Delivery runs Monday to Saturday, and assembly is available on the day for beds, sofas and dining sets.

What Makes Us Different

We're not trying to be everything to everyone. We focus on doing a few things very well.

Cash on Delivery

You only pay when your furniture arrives. Inspect it first, pay after. This is standard across all five countries we serve — Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.

Free delivery in Belgium

Most of our beds, sofas and dining sets qualify. For other countries, a small surcharge applies — always shown upfront before you order.

All sizes in stock

Single, double, king and super king beds. Sofas in compact and large configurations. We don't make you wait months for the size you actually need.

Weekly delivery routes

We run structured Monday–Saturday delivery routes. When you order, you get a real delivery day — not a vague two-week window.

Assembly available

Divan beds, luxury bed frames, gas-lift beds, dining sets and 3-seater sofas can all be fully assembled by our team on delivery day for a fixed fee.

Real customer support

We're reachable via Facebook, Instagram and email before and after your order. No ticket queues — just a straightforward conversation with the people handling your delivery.
