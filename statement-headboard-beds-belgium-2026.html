Statement Headboard Beds – 2026's Biggest Bedroom Trend in Belgium

Published July 22, 2026  ·  By Vantage Home  ·  7 min read

If there is one bedroom trend defining Belgian and Dutch homes in 2026, it is the statement headboard. A tall, upholstered headboard turns the bed into the centrepiece of the room — adding warmth, texture and a sense of luxury without needing any extra furniture. Here is how to choose one, and which style suits your space.

  4. Colours that work in 2026

1. Why Statement Headboards Are Trending in 2026

Bedroom design in Belgium and the Netherlands has moved away from plain, minimal frames towards warmer, more characterful rooms. The idea is simple: instead of filling a bedroom with lots of separate pieces, you let one bold element set the tone. A large upholstered headboard does exactly that.

There are a few practical reasons the trend has taken off. A tall headboard adds instant texture and softness to a room, which suits the warm, cosy interiors that are popular right now. It also works in almost any size of bedroom — in a large room it fills the wall beautifully, and in a compact Belgian apartment it gives a small space a designed, finished feel without taking up any extra floor area.

Finally, a velvet headboard brings a sense of quiet luxury that used to only come from expensive designer furniture. Today you can get the same look at a fraction of the price, delivered straight to your door.

2. The Three Statement Headboard Styles

Not every statement headboard looks the same. At Vantage Home there are three main styles, each with a very different personality.

Butterfly Bed

The butterfly bed is the boldest option. Its headboard flares outward at the top like a pair of wings, creating a dramatic, hotel-suite feel. It is the right choice if you want your bed to be the first thing anyone notices when they walk into the room. It pairs best with neutral walls and simple bedside furniture so the headboard can do the talking. Butterfly beds start from €520 including mattress.

Sleigh Bed

The sleigh bed offers a softer, more classical kind of drama. Its headboard and footboard curve gently outward, echoing the shape of a traditional sleigh. Finished in deep-button crushed velvet, it feels elegant and timeless rather than loud. This is the style to choose if you want a room that feels refined and calming. Sleigh beds start from €475 including mattress.

Chesterfield / Hilton Bed

The Chesterfield-style bed — sometimes called the Hilton — is defined by its deep button-tufted headboard, the same detailing found on classic Chesterfield sofas. It strikes a balance between the drama of the butterfly and the calm of the sleigh: tall and impressive, but structured and tailored. It suits both modern and more traditional bedrooms and is a safe choice if you are unsure which style fits your taste.

Quick guide: Choose the butterfly for maximum impact, the sleigh for classic elegance, and the Chesterfield/Hilton for a tailored look that works almost anywhere.

3. How to Choose the Right One for Your Room

Start with the size of your bedroom. In a larger room, a tall butterfly or Chesterfield headboard fills the wall and balances the space. In a smaller room, the same tall headboard still works — it draws the eye upward and makes the room feel more considered — but you may want to skip a footboard so the bed feels less bulky.

Next, think about storage. If your bedroom is short on space, choose a bed with a gas-lift base. The whole mattress lifts to reveal a large storage area underneath — enough for bedding, suitcases and seasonal clothes — so the bed doubles as a wardrobe. This is one of the most practical upgrades you can make in a compact Belgian apartment.

Finally, match the bed to how much attention you want it to command. A butterfly bed is a showpiece; a sleigh bed is a quiet statement. Both are on-trend for 2026 — it simply depends on the mood you want in the room.

4. Colours That Work in 2026

Belgian and Dutch bedrooms in 2026 are moving towards warm, calming tones. Grey is still popular and remains a safe, versatile choice that works with almost any bedding. But warmer shades are on the rise: soft beige, cream and warm taupe create a cosy, restful feel, while deeper tones like forest green or navy make a headboard feel rich and enveloping.

If you want your room to feel current for the next few years, a warm neutral or a deep jewel tone is the safer bet over cool, stark greys. Whichever you choose, velvet adds depth and catches the light beautifully, which is part of why it has become the go-to fabric for statement beds.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What is a statement headboard bed?

A bed with a large, eye-catching headboard that acts as the focal point of the room. Popular styles include the winged butterfly, the curved sleigh and the button-tufted Chesterfield, usually finished in velvet.

Why are statement headboards trending in 2026?

Belgian and Dutch bedrooms in 2026 favour warm, atmospheric rooms anchored by one bold piece. A tall upholstered headboard adds texture, colour and luxury without extra furniture, and works in both large and compact rooms.

Which statement headboard bed is best for a small bedroom?

A sleigh or Chesterfield bed with a gas-lift storage base. You get a striking headboard plus hidden storage underneath, so the bed replaces the need for extra drawers or a chest.
