Bed Frames, Sofas & Dining Tables Delivered Across Belgium

Looking for a bed frame with storage that actually fits a Belgian apartment? Or a corner sofa wide enough for the whole family? That's exactly what we focus on. At Vantage Home, we stock upholstered bed frames, platform beds, divan bases and gas-lift storage beds in every standard size — from 90×200 single to 180×200 super king — and deliver them directly to your door across Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.

Our divan bed with drawers range starts from €335, and every bed includes a quality mattress unless you choose otherwise. Whether you need a low bed frame for a minimalist bedroom or a dramatic sleigh bed with curved headboard, we've got you covered without the showroom markup.

On the sofa side, we carry a compact 3-seater sofa bed with storage and a large 7-seater sectional sofa with adjustable headrests — both built for everyday family life. All colours and configurations available on request.

Our dining table sets seat six comfortably — choose between a Turkish-style printed glass top or a luxury black and gold extendable design. Assembly is available on delivery day for a fixed fee, so you don't have to figure anything out yourself.

Wardrobes come in sliding mirror and 3-door mirror styles, available in white, grey and black. Priced from €450 and customer self-assembly, they're a straightforward upgrade to any bedroom.

Everything we sell ships from our warehouse on a structured weekly route — Monday through Saturday, by postcode. Cash on Delivery on every order. You inspect the furniture before you hand over a cent. That's how we deliver with confidence across Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.
