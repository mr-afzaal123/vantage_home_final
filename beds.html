Frequently Asked Questions – Bed Frames Belgium

Do the beds come with a mattress?

Yes — every bed frame includes a quality mattress in the price. No extra purchase needed. If you want a different mattress type, we sell them separately too.

What sizes are available?

Single (90×200), Double (140×200), King (160×200) and Super King (180×200). All four sizes are in stock. Divan beds come in all four; panel, sleigh and butterfly start at double.

Is delivery free in Belgium?

Yes, delivery in Belgium is always free. Other countries (Netherlands, Luxembourg, France, Germany) have a €30 surcharge, shown clearly before you order.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes — all orders are Cash on Delivery. You inspect the bed when it arrives. If you're not happy, you don't pay. Available in Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.

What is a divan bed?

A divan bed has a padded upholstered headboard and a solid base, often with storage drawers underneath. It's the most popular style for Belgian apartments due to its compact, clean look.

Do you assemble the bed on delivery?

Yes — assembly is available on delivery day for a fixed fee: +€30 for divan beds, +€40 for panel, sleigh and butterfly beds, +€100 if you add the optional gas-lift upgrade.
