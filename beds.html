Frequently Asked Questions – Bed Frames Belgium

Do the beds come with a mattress?

Yes — every bed frame includes a quality mattress in the price. No extra purchase needed. If you want a different mattress type, we sell them separately too.

What sizes are available?

Single (90x200), Double (140x200), King (160x200) and Super King (180x200). All four sizes are in stock. Divan beds come in all four; panel, sleigh and butterfly start at double.

Is delivery free in Belgium?

Yes, delivery in Belgium is always free. Other countries (Netherlands, Luxembourg, France, Germany) have a 30 EUR surcharge, shown clearly before you order.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes — all orders are Cash on Delivery. You inspect the bed when it arrives. If you are not happy, you do not pay. Available in Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.

What is a divan bed?

A divan bed has a padded upholstered headboard and a solid base, often with storage drawers underneath. It is the most popular style for Belgian apartments due to its compact, clean look.

Do you assemble the bed on delivery?

Yes — assembly is available on delivery day for a fixed fee: +30 EUR for divan beds, +40 EUR for panel, sleigh and butterfly beds, +100 EUR if you add the optional gas-lift upgrade.

How much does a bed frame cost in Belgium?

Bed frames at Vantage Home start from 335 EUR for a single divan bed, rising to 600 EUR for a super king butterfly bed — see the full price table above for every style and size. Every price includes a mattress, and delivery in Belgium is always free.
