Frequently Asked Questions

Do mattresses come with free delivery in Belgium?

Yes. All mattress orders in Belgium are delivered free of charge. For Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany a surcharge of €30 applies. Cash on Delivery on every order.

What mattress sizes are available?

We stock all standard European sizes: 90×200 cm (single), 140×200 cm (double), 160×200 cm (king) and 180×200 cm (super king). All three mattress types are available in every size.

What is the difference between the pocket spring and foam mattress?

The pocket spring mattress has individually wrapped springs that reduce motion transfer — ideal for couples. The foam mattress is a solid memory foam design with a quilted top, good for back support. The Turkish pocket spring is a mid-range option combining spring support with a padded top layer.

Can I buy a mattress separately or only with a bed?

You can buy our mattresses separately without a bed. All beds also come with a mattress included in the price — but if you already have a bed frame and only need a mattress, simply order one on its own.

Do you deliver mattresses to the Netherlands?

Yes. We deliver mattresses to the Netherlands with a €30 surcharge. Cash on Delivery available. Postcodes starting with 7xxx or 8xxx have a €50 surcharge due to distance.
