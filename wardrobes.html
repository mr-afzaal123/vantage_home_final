Frequently Asked Questions

Do wardrobes come with free delivery in Belgium?

Yes. All wardrobe orders in Belgium are delivered free. For Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany a surcharge of €30 applies (€50 for NL postcodes 7xxx/8xxx). Cash on Delivery on every order.

What wardrobe styles are available?

We stock two wardrobe styles: a 3-door wardrobe with full-length mirror panels and soft-close hinges, and a sliding door wardrobe with mirror front panels. Both available in white, grey and black.

What are the dimensions of the wardrobes?

The 3-door mirror wardrobe is approximately 150cm wide × 200cm tall × 60cm deep. The sliding door wardrobe is approximately 180cm wide × 200cm tall × 60cm deep. Exact dimensions are shown on the product detail page.

Is assembly included with wardrobe delivery?

Wardrobes are customer self-assembly and come with all hardware and instructions included. Professional assembly is not currently available for wardrobes.

Can I pay cash on delivery for a wardrobe in Belgium?

Yes. Every Vantage Home order is Cash on Delivery. You inspect the wardrobe boxes on arrival before paying. If packaging is damaged you can refuse delivery.
